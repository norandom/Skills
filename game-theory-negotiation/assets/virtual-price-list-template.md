# Virtual price list

Put a money value on every non-price term before the session. That gives you room to trade across issues, price late add-ons, and increase the total deal value.

The leverage is in asymmetric valuations: give what is cheap for you but valuable to them; take what is valuable to you but cheap for them.

## How to use
1. Trade, don't just discount. Concede items where their value exceeds your cost in exchange for items where your value exceeds their cost. Each good swap increases the surplus.
2. Price late add-ons. When a non-price demand appears late, read your cost from this list, attach the price, and isolate it from the agreed core: "happy to include it; that adds EUR X."
3. Stock the closing sweetener. Pick a low-cost, high-value item as the in-kind token to land the degressive close.
4. Verify win-win. At the end, confirm both sides ended above BATNA and that no high-asymmetry trade was left unused.
