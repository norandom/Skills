# Ratios and financial analysis

Two analysis lenses (common-size and trend) plus the ratio library and the 6-step interpretation workflow. Pair the mechanics here with the asset `assets/ratio-dashboard.md`.

## Common-size (vertical) analysis

Express every line as a % of a base: income statement → % of revenue; balance sheet → % of total assets. Reveals **structure and composition**. Monitor each category's share (e.g. rent ≤ 5% of revenue, fixed costs ~10%). Large deviation from a stable share signals an unreliable model or a real shift. Standardized percentages enable apples-to-apples comparison across companies of different sizes and against industry benchmarks. Meaningful comparisons: across time, between peers, against industry.

## Horizontal (trend) analysis

Absolute change and % change across periods or against budget. Identifies improving vs deteriorating trends — revenue growth rate, salary growth rate, actual vs budget. Used for growth analysis and forecasting.

## Breakeven

Minimum sales (or units) to cover total costs. Below it the entity loses money; above it it profits.

## Ratio library

**Liquidity**
- Current = Current Assets / Current Liabilities (benchmark 1–3; very high = idle assets)
- Quick = (Current Assets − Inventory) / Current Liabilities (≥ 1)
- Cash = Cash / Current Liabilities
- Net Working Capital = AR + Inventory − AP

**Profitability**
- Gross / EBITDA / Net margins (each ÷ Revenue)
- ROE = Net Income / Equity (ignores debt; best for equity-dominant sectors — IT, services, finance)
- ROA = Net Profit / Total Assets (asset utilization; compare same-industry)
- ROCE = EBIT / (Equity + LT debt)
- ROIC = EBIT × (1 − tax) / Total invested capital
- **ROE vs ROIC:** ROE is shareholders only; ROIC/ROCE cover all capital (equity + debt). ROIC is more comprehensive for a mixed debt/equity structure; capital-intensive sectors lean on it.
- **DuPont** (general knowledge, not in Bojan sources): ROE = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier — decomposes whether ROE comes from margins, asset use, or leverage.

**Efficiency**
- Asset Turnover = Revenue / Total Assets
- DSO = Avg AR / Revenue × 365; DIO = Avg Inventory / COGS × 365; DPO = Avg AP / COGS × 365
- CCC = DSO + DIO − DPO (lower = better)

**Leverage**
- Equity Ratio = Equity / (Total Equity + Liabilities); Debt Ratio = Debt / (Total Equity + Liabilities)
- EBITDA-to-Interest Coverage = EBITDA / Interest expense

**Market multiples:** P/E, P/S, P/B, EV/EBITDA, P/CF, Dividend Yield.

## Diagnostic reading (always compare vs history / budget / competitors)

- EBITDA margin healthy but **net margin lagging** → the problem is below the operating line: interest expense (financing policy) and/or corporate income tax (tax policy).
- **ROA falls year-over-year** → assets growing faster than net income; red flag unless the company is in an early-growth investment phase.
- **Declining CCC = efficient** (low DSO + low DIO + high DPO retains cash). But excessively high DPO forfeits early-payment discounts. Example: DSO of 16 days beating both budget and competitors is favorable.

## The 6-step ratio workflow

1. Gather data (balance sheet + P&L). 2. Understand the ratio. 3. Apply the formula. 4. Interpret. 5. Comparative read (vs last period / budget / competitors). 6. Risk assessment and action plan.
