# Budgeting and planning

A budget translates strategy into a financial plan with accountability. This reference covers the four budgeting methods (and when to use each), the budget-vs-forecast-vs-plan distinctions, and the cost-driver matrix that turns each expense line into a formula.

Notes layer: personal notes about `Budgeting Handbook.pdf`, `Cost Budgeting Handboook.pdf`, `Budgeting model.xlsx`, and `Budgeting vs Rolling Forecast.pdf` from Bojan Radojicic / bojanfin.com materials. This is not original source text. Pair with `assets/budget-model.md`.

## The budgeting flow

Strategy → financial goals → purpose → team / milestones → operational inputs → build & monitor → communicate → review & approval.

---

## Four budgeting methods

| Method | How | Pro / Con | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero-Based (ZBB)** | Build from scratch; justify every expense | Thoughtful / time-consuming | Cutting legacy cost, reallocating to strategy |
| **Incremental** | Prior baseline +/- adjustment | Fast / carries inefficiency forward | Stable, predictable firms |
| **Continuous / Rolling** | Append a new period to keep a constant horizon (e.g. 12 mo) | Flexible / ongoing effort | Dynamic environments |
| **Beyond Budgeting** | Abandon fixed annual targets for relative/dynamic KPIs + dynamic resource allocation | Adaptive / cultural change needed | Volatile industries |

---

## Budget vs rolling forecast vs plan

| | Budget | Rolling forecast | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizon | Fixed fiscal year | 3–6 quarters ahead, updated quarterly | Long-term strategy |
| Detail | Granular line items | KPI-level | Broad (investment / debt / capital allocation / risk) |
| Purpose | Accountability | Early warning, navigate uncertainty | Direction, adaptable |

## The cost-driver matrix

Each cost line gets a **driver → calculation → data source**. This is what makes a budget defensible rather than a guess.

| Cost line | Calculation | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| COGS | GP rate on sales (drivers: volume, GP rate, purchase prices, inflation, discount policy, seasonality) | Sales plan |
| Direct labor | Headcount × avg salary × % increase | HR / CEO |
| Consultancy | % of revenue or contracts | Contracts |
| Repairs & maintenance | % of revenue or direct costs | GL |
| Utilities / telephone / rent / office | YoY growth vs baseline | Trial balance / GL |
| D&A | Useful life / rates + new investment | FA register + CAPEX schedule |
| Inventory scrap | % of COGS or % of avg inventory | GL |
| Bad-debt impairment | % of revenue or % of avg receivables | GL |
| Bonuses | % of salaries or % of net profit | HR |
| Marketing | % of budgeted revenue | Plan |
| Travel / licenses / R&D / insurance | Historical × growth | GL |
| Interest | Roll-forward on loan schedules | Financing schedule |
| Tax | Effective rate on NIBT, adjusted for incentives | Tax plan |

---

## Budgeting model mechanics

Per line: **Starting position | assumption % | 12 forecast periods | Total**. Variable costs are projected as a **share of revenue** (historical %, user-revisable). The result flows to Net Income, then to the Balance Sheet. PPE / fixed assets link to a **separate CAPEX model** — keep schedules modular.
