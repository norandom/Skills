# Degressive concession plan

Pre-compute your concession schedule so you do not improvise toward your floor. Each step halves the remaining distance to your target price. Numbers get smaller and more precise. Finish with an in-kind sweetener, not more price.

## Discipline checklist
- [ ] Target sits in the middle of the range, not at my reservation price.
- [ ] First concession is relatively large (good-faith signal).
- [ ] Steps shrink and numbers get precise/odd. Do not land exactly on the midpoint every time; they'll detect the algorithm.
- [ ] Don't over-round; precise figures read as "real calculated limit."
- [ ] Reserve the closing in-kind sweetener; deploy it when monetary steps shrink to noise. Timing matters. Omitting it can sour the deal.
- [ ] Default to degressive, not Boulwarism. No take-it-or-leave-it unless I have a dominant BATNA and accept breakdown risk.
